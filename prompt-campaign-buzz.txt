PROMPT — Build "Campaign Buzz": a multi-tenant email campaign platform
(customers, templates, bulk send with variables, schedules, SMTP, analytics, customer images, BVN, contact list, auto-reply image)
================================================================================

Build a NEW application called Campaign Buzz. It is NOT a patch to
mailmergepromain — that project is only a REFERENCE for patterns (listed in §2).

The product in one sentence: users sign in with email + password, import their
customers from JSON (or add them by hand), keep reusable templates full of
variables, compose and send personalised emails from an Inbox, schedule sends,
and watch delivery/open analytics on a dashboard. Customers can have an
individual profile image, a BVN, and multiple contacts (contact list) with full
details; when messaging a contact, communication is always in respect of the
actual (parent) customer, and the contact appears in chat/list contexts as
"contact - (parent name)".
